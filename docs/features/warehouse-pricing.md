# Warehouse pricing

The operator sets workspace-wide defaults at `/warehouse/pricing`. CRM account → Warehouse stores customer-specific rates. An effective customer rate replaces the default with the same charge code; all other default codes remain inherited. A zero-price override with zero minimum explicitly waives a charge. Removing or expiring an override restores the default. Codes must not overlap within a card, including the inclusive end date.

Each charge has a stage (goods in, storage, goods out, transaction), measurement (pallet, unit, cubic metre, kilogram or fixed), currency, rate, minimum, effective dates and, for storage, time period and free periods. Booking fees apply per booking; transaction fees per occurrence. Storage supports nights, exact hours, started 24-hour periods or started seven-night periods. Free periods use the chosen frequency. Minimums apply per booking/transaction or per storage stay, never when quantity or chargeable time is zero. Amounts are net estimates; currencies are totalled separately.

The preview is an explicit constant-stock scenario using operator-supplied nights/hours and counts. The chosen pricing date fixes rates for the entire scenario. It is not a historical inventory reconstruction, tax calculation, invoice, billing run or automatic posting. Partial receipts/releases require actual stock-event quantities in a future billing adapter. No arbitrary drag-and-drop branches are introduced: the four stages are a keyboard-accessible visual guide into the rate editor.

Persistence uses private, company-scoped versioned rate cards and atomic audit history, accessed only through an authenticated permission-checking RPC. `Warehouse.Read` or `Warehouse.Write` permits internal reads; `Warehouse.Write` permits changes. CRM scope is also verified for customer cards. Direct table and external customer access are denied. Both card and inherited-default versions protect against stale saves.

Existing WMS service contracts, storage rules and billing events are not rewritten. Those are legacy operational billing records; these rate cards are the operator's pricing configuration, pending a reviewed event-rating adapter. Before enabling billing, agree warehouse/facility-specific terms, night cutoff/time zone, partial-stock history, contract price locking, billing cycle, tax/charge-code mapping, rounding at invoice level, dispute/reversal handling and idempotent posting into existing WMS billing events. Never infer pallets from SKU units without a verified handling-unit conversion.

## Dexter exception

Chat reads, writes and Watching for you for these new pricing cards are explicitly unsupported until a typed pricing adapter can distinguish configuration, estimates and posted charges. Existing warehouse movement/order capabilities cannot truthfully represent a rate change. Both chat and watch prompts and capability descriptions direct operators to Warehouse pricing or CRM account → Warehouse; neither may substitute a generic account/warehouse watch or claim to save prices. The rate cards themselves are audited. This is a documented temporary parity exception, not an implicit promise of billing automation.
