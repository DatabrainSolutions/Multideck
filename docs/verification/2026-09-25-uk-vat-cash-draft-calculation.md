# UK Cash Accounting draft calculation

The draft calculation records an immutable revision for one Cash VAT period and one current, verified payment-event projection. Its Box 1, 4, 6 and 7 source lines retain individual payment-event IDs and four-decimal amounts, including multiple part payments against one invoice. The calculation records a projection fingerprint, registration terms and rounded draft boxes. Another source revision creates another calculation rather than editing a prior one.

The draft remains **unreconciled**. It cannot approve or submit a Cash return. Cash period creation and registration changes remain guarded off in the product. The required [Cash VAT control bridge](../architecture/uk-vat-cash-control-bridge.md) defines the ledger-to-payment identity and sign-off gates. Before enabling Cash periods, the workflow still needs:

- a complete cash-to-VAT-control bridge, including credits, refunds, excluded transactions and transitions;
- individual Cash event reconciliation dates and source locks, plus a review lock that checks all event lines and exceptions;
- proof that earlier Cash events were included in accepted returns, so an exit adjustment cannot count them again;
- HMRC obligation matching, sandbox end-to-end testing, filing receipt/readback and production approval.

The calculator and control-source inventory are service-only database APIs reached through permissioned Finance Edge preparation routes. Dexter chat and Watching for you deliberately do not expose them yet: there is no operator-approved Cash return lifecycle to inspect or watch, and presenting these previews as a return would mislead operators. Dexter must report Cash filing as unsupported until the full lifecycle is available.

The read-only Cash control source inventory now binds the invoice-balance snapshot and verified payment projection to the same recorded VAT period. A separate exact-decimal bridge preview checks the opening unpaid VAT plus invoice VAT posted in the period, less closing unpaid VAT, against period payment-event VAT for output and input tax separately. Date anomalies, credits, missing evidence, unsupported cash and arithmetic differences block the preview. The inventory verifies each inventoried invoice VAT line against its native posted journal line and catches tax postings on those invoices without a matching source line. It requires every accounting period covering the VAT period to have a current approved monthly VAT control; missing, overlapping or stale months block the preview. The movement inventory compares tax-labelled and VAT-control postings in the period with current-period invoice VAT sources. Earlier-period invoice tax stays outside the current movement total. A verified opening-balance batch is listed separately; an unexplained posting or partial accounting month blocks the preview. The accepted-history inventory requires contiguous prior Cash periods and an accepted production return for each, proved by an HMRC receipt or matching readback. Every earlier invoice payment line must be present in that return's immutable Cash event calculation. The full VAT-control inventory now calculates a deterministic opening balance, period movement and closing balance for every VAT nominal and classifies historical as well as current postings. The bridge checks that the balance changes match source invoice VAT, unpaid timing and accepted prior Cash net due. Accepted Cash-return settlements can now be prepared by one authorised operator and posted by another against a matching bank statement line. The native posting debits or credits VAT control and balances the bank account; bank reconciliation and the Cash bridge recognise that exact posting. Unlinked and unaccepted settlements remain blocked. It still needs the two-person VAT-period lock and never sets `returnReady` true.

The protected Finance Edge API now serves the complete Cash control inventory and exact-decimal bridge preview for a selected period and projection, and records an unreconciled Cash draft through the native calculation RPC. It checks compliance permissions, company/legal-entity scope and identifiers before calling the service-only database functions. Client API methods expose both operations. The pure bridge tests, client TypeScript build and Edge TypeScript syntax parse pass locally; an authenticated browser journey, Cash review lock and HMRC filing remain unverified. Dexter chat and Watching for you continue to report Cash filing as unsupported.

Cash payment-event reconciliation is now a separate sign-off from Standard invoice evidence. Its immutable record holds the server reconciliation timestamp and operator, the exact calculation digest, projection, payment allocation and invoice line. A protected Finance route recomputes the current Cash draft under source locks before it can sign up to 100 distinct events; a separate scoped, paged read returns the stored dates and source links later. The first sign-off for an invoice activates the existing document and journal locks; signed payment dates and tax decisions cannot be revised. Later part payments can receive their own reconciliation dates. The database rejects a changed draft, another legal entity, a second projection of the same allocation and direct table writes. This remains a preparation workflow: no Cash period review lock, filing approval or HMRC submission is enabled. Dexter chat and Watching for you explicitly treat Cash event sign-off as unsupported until a complete operator lifecycle and safe event adapter exist; they must not claim Cash filing readiness.
