# Cash Accounting exit invoice inventory — local verification

HMRC [VAT Notice 731, section 6.4](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/vat-cash-accounting-scheme-notice-731) requires outstanding VAT to be accounted for on leaving Cash Accounting. That includes a supply with no payment at all. The payment-driven period preview cannot enumerate those invoices.

The service-only `multideck_uk_vat_cash_exit_invoice_inventory` RPC starts from every posted sales and purchase invoice whose document date falls between supplied entry and exit dates for one UK GBP legal entity. It returns all invoices, including wholly unpaid ones, with posted allocations through exit, future allocations, unsupported allocation counts, source exceptions, an exact source digest and an all-or-nothing extraction limit. The digest binds invoice balance fields and individual payment/allocation sources. It now also includes every posted credit or debit note in that interval, even without an invoice link or cash allocation, and changes the digest when one appears. Any such note needs a reviewed price-change/refund path before an exit calculation may be used. Read access uses the existing legal-entity VAT check; only the service role can invoke the RPC.

This is a **balance inventory**, not a Cash exit calculation or return. The caller must verify the actual scheme entry and final Cash period, tax points, reviewed VAT decisions, prior accepted Standard returns, credit/refund events, source line totals, HMRC exclusion and transition rules, and the chosen immediate or six-month exit option. An invoice dated outside the supplied interval is outside this inventory. No Edge route, operator action, VAT evidence, posting, period lock or filing is enabled. Dexter chat and Watching for you cannot inspect or watch this service-only preflight and must continue to report Cash exit as unsupported.

Verification: the disposable PostgreSQL fixture proves a wholly unpaid sale remains visible, a partly paid purchase separates pre-exit and future allocations, a foreign-currency invoice is flagged, an unreviewed payment and a posted credit note each change the digest, more than 1,000 invoices yield no partial list, and foreign/read-only callers are denied. The staged tenant VAT migration chain passes locally. The serial access regression passed after the price-change guard: 121 database and 9 contract checks, zero failures.
