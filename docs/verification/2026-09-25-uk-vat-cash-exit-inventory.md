# Cash Accounting exit invoice inventory — local verification

HMRC [VAT Notice 731, section 6.4](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/vat-cash-accounting-scheme-notice-731) requires outstanding VAT to be accounted for on leaving Cash Accounting. That includes a supply with no payment at all. The payment-driven period preview cannot enumerate those invoices.

The service-only `multideck_uk_vat_cash_exit_invoice_inventory` RPC starts from every posted sales and purchase invoice whose document date falls between supplied entry and exit dates for one UK GBP legal entity. It returns all invoices, including wholly unpaid ones, with posted allocations through exit, future allocations, unsupported allocation counts, source exceptions, an exact source digest and an all-or-nothing extraction limit. The digest binds invoice balance fields and individual payment/allocation sources. It also includes every posted credit or debit note in that interval, even without an invoice link or cash allocation, and changes the digest when one appears. Any such note needs a reviewed price-change/refund path before an exit calculation may be used. A further read-only layer attaches every invoice line, its native VAT evidence and latest reviewed decision. Missing/non-Cash treatment, a mismatched source amount, a linked credit and prior Standard/Annual sign-off become line-source issues; each is bound to the digest. The exit interval now also inventories posted, reversed and pending cash using transaction, accounting and latest reviewed payment dates. Unsupported cash types, reversed/pending status, unallocated or unmatched amounts, absent payment reviews, non-GBP sources and posting after exit become cash-source issues and change the digest. Read access uses the existing legal-entity VAT check; only the service role can invoke the RPC.

This is a **source inventory**, not a Cash exit calculation or return. The caller must verify the actual scheme entry and final Cash period, payment-review fingerprints, tax points, prior filed Cash events, credit/refund treatment, HMRC exclusion and transition rules, the native control bridge, and the chosen immediate or six-month exit option. An invoice dated outside the supplied interval is outside this inventory. No Edge route, operator action, VAT evidence, posting, period lock or filing is enabled. A posted refund, reversed receipt and payment review backdated into the exit interval are included and flagged.
