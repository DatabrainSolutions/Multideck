# Cash Accounting exit invoice inventory — local verification

HMRC [VAT Notice 731, section 6.4](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/vat-cash-accounting-scheme-notice-731) requires outstanding VAT to be accounted for on leaving Cash Accounting. That includes a supply with no payment at all. The payment-driven period preview cannot enumerate those invoices.

The service-only `multideck_uk_vat_cash_exit_invoice_inventory` RPC starts from every posted sales and purchase invoice whose document date falls between supplied entry and exit dates for one UK GBP legal entity. It returns all invoices, including wholly unpaid ones, with posted allocations through exit, future allocations, unsupported allocation counts, source exceptions, an exact source digest and an all-or-nothing extraction limit. The digest binds invoice balance fields and individual payment/allocation sources. It also includes every posted credit or debit note in that interval, even without an invoice link or cash allocation, and changes the digest when one appears. Any such note needs a reviewed price-change/refund path before an exit calculation may be used. A further read-only layer attaches every invoice line, its native VAT evidence and latest reviewed decision. Missing/non-Cash treatment, a mismatched source amount, a linked credit and prior Standard/Annual sign-off become line-source issues; each is bound to the digest. The exit interval also inventories posted, reversed and pending cash using transaction, accounting and latest reviewed payment dates. Unsupported cash types, reversed/pending status, unallocated or unmatched amounts, absent payment reviews, non-GBP sources and posting after exit become cash-source issues and change the digest. The latest payment-date review fingerprint is now recomputed from the full cash row and every allocation using the same formula as the review writer; a mismatch is a source issue and changes the digest. Read access uses the existing legal-entity VAT check; only the service role can invoke the RPC.

All invoice, line, evidence, price-change, cash and allocation monetary fields now leave the RPC as decimal strings (`amountEncoding: decimal_strings`). PostgreSQL `numeric` amounts remain exact across JSON parsing in JavaScript; counts and status fields keep their previous types. The source digest covers the encoded source values as well as the prior source digest. Any future calculation must use decimal arithmetic on those strings and reject an unexpected encoding.

The service-only `multideck_uk_vat_cash_exit_verified_inventory` entry point now derives the inventory dates from a closed UK Cash registration and its final Cash period. The period must end on the registration exit date; a consecutive Standard registration with the same VAT number must begin the next day. The result includes the exact registration and period IDs, term timestamps, and a digest that binds this transition context to the source inventory. Caller-supplied dates cannot substitute for this verified entry point. The lower-level date-based inventory remains service-only for source diagnostics and must not be used alone for a transition calculation.

The inventory also carries each invoice's native due date and a six-month eligibility flag. A missing due date, a date before invoice issue or payment due beyond six months sets `source_exception` and increments `dueTermIssueCount`; the date and issue count change the digest. This mirrors the Cash payment preview's exclusion check and prevents an immediate-exit calculation from treating an ineligible invoice as a Cash invoice. Other statutory exclusions still require explicit evidence and review.

Each reviewed invoice line now includes its decision tax point, an in-term flag and whether its treatment is among the supported Cash sale or purchase treatments. A Cash-labelled reverse charge, import, other unsupported treatment or out-of-term tax point increments `lineTreatmentIssueCount` and the combined line issue count. These fields are bound to the source digest. This is a conservative review blocker, not a claim that the supported treatment list covers every statutory Cash scenario.

The backend-only `previewUkVatCashExitImmediate` now consumes a complete verified inventory and calculates the unpaid fraction for each reviewed invoice with exact four-decimal arithmetic. It returns traceable invoice-line contributions and aggregate candidate source amounts for Boxes 1, 4, 6 and 7. Any source issue, missing amount encoding, inconsistent balance, stale payment review, unreviewed price change or nonzero apportionment remainder removes all box totals rather than presenting a partial result. Its `returnReady` value is always false.

This is an **immediate-option source preview**, not a Cash exit return. The verified transition context establishes recorded term dates, but does not establish that prior Cash returns were accepted or that the final Cash period is ready to file. The caller must still verify prior filed Cash events, credit/refund treatment, remaining HMRC exclusions and transition rules, the native control bridge, and the chosen immediate or six-month exit option. An invoice dated outside the recorded Cash term is outside this inventory. No Edge route, operator action, VAT evidence, posting, period lock or filing is enabled. Dexter chat and Watching for you cannot inspect or watch this backend-only preflight and must continue to report Cash exit as unsupported. Existing registration-change rules still require prior prepared periods to be accepted, and Cash period creation remains disabled; that lifecycle must be implemented before operators can use this entry point.

A posted refund, reversed receipt and payment review backdated into the exit interval are included and flagged.
