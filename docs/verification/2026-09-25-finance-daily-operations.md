# Finance daily operations: local verification

## Delivered locally

- Supplier purchase orders are separate from customer warehouse purchase orders. Operators record a commitment; a second Finance reviewer approves it. Supplier invoices can be matched to an approved PO only while the invoice is a draft or awaiting approval. The database checks legal entity, supplier, currency, job where both records have one, document net amount, remaining PO value, and duplicate invoice matches under row locks.
- Invoice matching offers deterministic, source-linked candidates. An authorised reviewer may optionally request an AI proposal. The model sees only the current invoice and eligible approved POs. The proposal stores the model and prompt versions, source document/PO revisions, retained invoice file identity and SHA-256 where available, source-field citations, rationale, status, reviewer and reason. Rejected proposals remain in history. A proposal cannot post an invoice; match approval rechecks the live records and links the proposal atomically. When AI is unavailable, the rule suggestions remain available.
- The aged receivables/payables worklists use approved open invoices, due dates and outstanding amounts. Signed open credit/debit notes and approved unapplied cash are shown separately and reduce the net total without silently assigning an offset to an invoice ageing bucket. Payment runs stop when that supplier/currency has an unapplied offset. Collection follow-ups are recorded against open customer invoices. Customer statements show current open invoices, credits and unapplied receipts with source IDs; they are not a historical transaction ledger or a sending workflow.
- A payment run groups selected approved supplier invoices by supplier and creates controlled cash drafts and allocations. Preparation requires a reason; a different Finance reviewer approves or rejects the complete run. Approval uses the existing native cash-posting lifecycle and its accounting configuration checks. Remittance advice is a printable reviewed draft; Multideck does not transmit the advice or initiate a bank transfer from this screen.
- Job profitability reads the canonical charge profitability view and exposes its source charge lines. Dexter's `finance_operations` domain and deterministic watch signals cover supplier POs, AI match proposals, payment runs and collection actions. Daily operational writes remain in the Finance workspaces; Dexter must describe them as manual controls.

## Release limits

- A supplier PO is a reviewed header commitment. Line-by-line three-way matching against goods receipt is outside this implementation.
- AI citation field keys are validated against canonical invoice/PO values, but model quality and false-positive rates still need evaluation against a reviewed supplier-invoice set before calling AI matching production-validated.
- Aged balances and statements are current-value views. Historical as-of statements need ledger snapshot or transaction replay work.
- Remittance sending, payment file export and bank execution are separate integrations and are not claimed here.
- Apply the migration and Edge function to the intended tenant only after the access regression and live, role-aware tenant preflight pass. This file does not claim a deployment.
