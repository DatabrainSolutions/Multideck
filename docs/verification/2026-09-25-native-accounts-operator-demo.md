# Native Accounts operator demo script

Status: prepared for Finance 5 acceptance. This is a runbook, not evidence that a browser journey or deployment has passed. Use the preview-scoped development deployment and its intended Supabase project after the release gates pass. Do not change the existing sandbox-linked legal entity or exercise an ERPNext mirror.

## Preflight

1. Confirm the exact app URL, deployed commit, Supabase project and migration/Edge versions. Verify that `finance-operations`, `finance-subledger`, `finance-ledger` and `finance-reconciliation` serve this same tenant.
2. Select the separately provisioned, clearly named **native-only** GBP legal entity with mirror policy `disabled`. Confirm an open accounting period, approved base currency and tax treatments, native bank/control nominals, one active payment bank, one supplier and one customer profile. Stop if any selection resolves to the existing sandbox-linked entity.
3. Use two real internal users: a maker with Finance draft/cash rights, and a different reviewer with `Finance.ReviewAndPost` and bank review rights. Use a job with exact buy and sell charge lines, approved effective nominal mappings, and no unrelated activity in the chosen bank period. Record the entity, bank, job, party and charge-line IDs in the evidence log. Do not use a public or production customer.
4. Use a unique demo reference prefix and one reviewed purchase tax treatment and sales tax treatment. The example net amounts are GBP 100 supplier cost and GBP 180 customer revenue. Record the actual tax and gross amounts returned by the product; do not assume a VAT rate. The illustrative expected gross profit is GBP 80 only if these are the job's only recognised revenue and cost and no open WIP/accrual changes it.

| 1. Supplier commitment | Maker opens **Finance → Suppliers & payables → Supplier purchase orders**, selects the native-only entity, supplier and job, and saves a GBP 100 net PO with the unique reference. Supplier bill and source-cited match | Maker follows **Supplier invoices**, creates a purchase invoice for the same supplier, GBP 100 net, with a reviewed tax treatment and the exact buy charge line. A proposal alone must not approve or post. |
| 3. Supplier settlement | Maker opens **Payment runs & remittances**, selects the approved bill, exact GBP bank, date, exchange rate 1 and preparation reason. Reviewer approves the run with a separate reason. Inspect the resulting supplier cash, allocation, AP balance and native posting. Open **Review remittance** as a printable draft only. | Run, item, cash, allocation and posting IDs; second reviewer; invoice outstanding balance before/after; no claim of bank transfer or sent remittance. |
| 4. Customer sale and receipt | Maker opens **Finance → Customers & receivables → Customer invoices**, creates a GBP 180 net sales invoice for the same job using the exact sell charge line and approved tax treatment. Maker sends it for review; reviewer posts it. Maker opens **Customer receipts & allocation**, records a GBP 60 receipt to the same bank, allocates exactly GBP 60 to the invoice, and sends it for review; reviewer approves. | Sales document, charge line, cash, allocation and native posting IDs; AR outstanding must fall by GBP 60 and retain the remaining gross balance. |
| 5. Bank control | Open **Finance → Cash & banking → Bank reconciliation** on the same entity, bank and complete open period. Import a CSV containing every relevant period movement, including the supplier payment as a negative amount and customer receipt as a positive amount; its opening, each running balance and closing must agree with the bank ledger. Leave one line unmatched first and record the **Incomplete** control. Match each line to the exact posted cash with reasons, then verify the period once the app shows **Ready for review**. | Statement and line IDs, cash matches, initial incomplete reason, posted bank ledger lines, verified opening/closing control, verifier and reason. Do not invent a short CSV for a bank period with other activity. |
| 6. Customer follow-up and statement | Open **Collections worklist** for the native-only entity. Inspect the aged customer invoice and record a follow-up with a date and reason. The statement is a current open-item view, not a historical ledger or a sent message. |
| 7. Profitability and native books | Open **Job profitability** on the same entity and expand the job's charge lines. Compare actual revenue/cost and recognised GP with the posted source documents. Open **General ledger → Journals** and **Financial reports** to trace the native posting batches and AR/AP/bank controls. | Job and charge-line IDs, expected/actual/WIP/accrual figures, native journal IDs, balanced debit/credit totals and report period. State any unexplained difference instead of adjusting a figure to fit the example. |

## Deliberate denial and recovery

- Try to approve the PO or payment run as its maker; it must be denied and remain awaiting the different reviewer.
- Leave one bank statement line unmatched and show that period verification stays incomplete. Complete the exact cash match and recheck; do not bypass the control.
- Present a supplier invoice with the wrong supplier, currency, job or net value to matching. The rule suggestion and database approval must reject it without changing the bill or PO.
- If AI is unavailable, show the recoverable message and source-cited deterministic candidates. If it is available, record a rejected false-positive proposal with a reviewer reason and verify that it cannot be attached to a match.
- Confirm a read-only colleague can inspect permitted records but cannot approve, and a foreign-entity request is denied without disclosing source detail.
