1. Confirm the exact app URL, deployed commit, Supabase project and migration/Edge versions. Verify that `finance-operations`, `finance-subledger`, `finance-ledger` and `finance-reconciliation` serve this same tenant.
2. Select the separately provisioned, clearly named **native-only** GBP legal entity with mirror policy `disabled`. 3. Use two real internal users: a maker with Finance draft/cash rights, and a different reviewer with `Finance.ReviewAndPost` and bank review rights for exceptions. Configure versioned policies on this legal entity for the intended low-risk `purchase_order`, `supplier_match`, `document`, `cash` and `payment_run` cases. Record each policy ID, revision, mode and amount cap; leave other policies at review unless separately demonstrated. Caps must cover the actual base-currency amounts including tax where applicable. Use a job with exact buy and sell charge lines, approved effective nominal mappings, and no unrelated activity in the chosen bank period. Record the entity, bank, job, party and charge-line IDs in the evidence log. Do not use a public or production customer.
4. Use a unique demo reference prefix and one reviewed purchase tax treatment and sales tax treatment. The example net amounts are GBP 100 supplier cost and GBP 180 customer revenue. Record the actual tax and gross amounts returned by the product; do not assume a VAT rate. The illustrative expected gross profit is GBP 80 only if these are the job's only recognised revenue and cost and no open WIP/accrual changes it.

## Deliberate denial and recovery

- With `always_review` on a separate test record, try to approve a PO or payment run as its maker; it must be denied and remain awaiting the different reviewer. A policy-approved automatic record instead carries the actor, policy ID/revision, limit and source evidence in the audit trail.
- Leave one bank statement line unmatched and show that period verification stays incomplete. Complete the exact cash match and recheck; do not bypass the control.
- Present a supplier invoice with the wrong supplier, currency, job or net value to matching. The rule suggestion and database approval must reject it without changing the bill or PO.
- If AI is unavailable, show the recoverable message and source-cited deterministic candidates. If it is available, record a rejected false-positive proposal with a reviewer reason and verify that it cannot be attached to a match.
- Confirm a read-only colleague can inspect permitted records but cannot approve, and a foreign-entity request is denied without disclosing source detail.
